Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Policy explains how Shenzhen Lingyi Heyuan Technology Co., Ltd. / ly origin (“we”) handles information when you use RoundBuddy.

Information stored on your device

RoundBuddy stores data locally to provide the app, including workout plans, training history, tags, an active unfinished session, preferences, user-entered weight and weight unit, AI recommendation cache, and Pro entitlement status, transaction identifier, last-check time, and sync metadata. Your weight is an app profile value; it is not read from Apple Health.

iCloud / CloudKit sync

While Pro entitlement is valid and iCloud is available, sync runs automatically and workout plans, tags, training history, deletion tombstones, and a device-scoped identifier may be written to the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. Apple provides the iCloud/CloudKit service under its own terms and privacy information.

There is currently no in-app switch to disable sync and no in-app control to erase all CloudKit data. You can delete individual workouts or tags in the app; those deletions are designed to sync. You may manage iCloud access or data through Apple’s account and device controls where available, or contact us to discuss available options. Removing the app alone does not guarantee CloudKit erasure.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app requests write access only to save a martial-arts workout and estimated active energy. It does not request or read body weight, heart rate, steps, or other HealthKit data. You can change Health permissions in Apple Health or Settings.

AI features and requests

When you use AI recommendations, the app may send locale; training goal or your custom text; selected duration and intensity; and up to five recent-session summaries (date, duration, completed stage count, and workout name). When you ask to generate a workout, it may send locale, your workout brief, duration, and intensity. For AI calorie refinement, it may send locale, your entered weight, completed duration, and workout titles, descriptions, and structure. In Release builds, a valid StoreKit entitlement JWS may be sent as an authentication header for eligible AI requests.

The current Release endpoint is configured on Volcengine Beijing API Gateway and forwards prompts to Volcengine Ark Responses with store: false. Current server code has no app database persistence and does not log request bodies or their content. Operational logs in the current code can include request/trace/provider request IDs, route, status, duration, and usage.

The current app and server source does not establish exact provider or infrastructure logging, retention, access, deletion, or regional/cross-border handling. We do not claim a specific retention period or processing location beyond the configured Beijing endpoints. Contact us for current information.

Purchases and third parties

Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. We do not receive full payment card information. The app reads entitlement information and locally caches only the subscription information described above. Our current source review found no third-party advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK. Dependencies and the App Store privacy answers must be reviewed again before release.

Retention and deletion

Local information generally remains until you delete individual data in the app or remove the app. CloudKit information follows individual item deletion/sync behavior and Apple’s iCloud management and account controls; the app does not currently provide a comprehensive CloudKit erase control. The current AI server code does not persist app data and requests provider non-storage, but infrastructure and provider log retention remains unconfirmed. Contact us if you need help understanding available options.

Children, updates, and contact

RoundBuddy is not directed to children in a way that knowingly collects their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us. We may update this Policy and will post a new date here.

Questions can be sent to luke@ly-origin.com. You can also read the RoundBuddy Terms of Service.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 23 日

本政策说明深圳领弈禾元科技有限公司 / ly origin（“我们”）在你使用拳伴（RoundBuddy）时如何处理信息。

保存在设备上的信息

为提供 App 功能，拳伴会在本机保存训练计划、训练历史、标签、未完成的活动训练、偏好设置、用户输入的体重及体重单位、AI 推荐缓存，以及 Pro 授权状态、交易标识、最后检查时间和同步元数据。体重是 App 内资料，不会从 Apple 健康读取。

iCloud / CloudKit 同步

当 Pro 授权有效且 iCloud 可用时，同步会自动运行；训练计划、标签、训练历史、删除墓碑记录和设备范围标识符可能会写入与你 Apple 账户关联的私有 CloudKit 数据库。iCloud/CloudKit 由 Apple 依据其自身条款和隐私说明提供。

当前 App 没有关闭同步的用户开关，也没有清除全部 CloudKit 数据的 App 内控制项。你可以在 App 内删除单个训练或标签，设计上会同步这些删除；可在 Apple 的账户和设备控制项中管理 iCloud 访问或数据（如该控制项可用），也可联系我们了解可用选项。仅卸载 App 并不保证 CloudKit 数据被清除。

Apple 健康

在你授权后，App 仅请求写入一条武术训练记录和估算的活动能量。它不会请求或读取体重、心率、步数或其他 HealthKit 数据。你可以在 Apple 健康或“设置”中更改健康权限。

AI 功能与请求

使用 AI 推荐时，App 可能提交语言、训练目标或你输入的自定义文字、所选时长与强度，以及最多五条近期训练摘要（日期、时长、完成阶段数和训练名称）。使用 AI 生成训练时，可能提交语言、训练简介、时长和强度。使用 AI 精细热量估算时，可能提交语言、你输入的体重、完成时长，以及训练标题、描述和结构。Release 版本对符合条件的 AI 请求还可能以认证请求头提交有效的 StoreKit entitlement JWS。

当前 Release 端点配置在火山引擎北京 API Gateway，并将提示词转发给火山引擎 Ark Responses，设置 store: false。当前服务端代码没有 App 数据库持久化，也不记录请求正文或内容；代码中的运行日志可能包含请求/trace/服务提供方请求 ID、路由、状态、时长和用量。

当前 App 和服务端源代码无法确定服务提供方或基础设施的确切日志、保存期限、访问、删除机制，以及跨地区或跨境处理。除已配置的北京端点外，我们不主张特定的保存期限或处理地点；如需当前信息，请联系我们。

购买与第三方

购买由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 和 App Store 处理，我们不会接触完整支付卡信息。App 会读取授权状态，并仅在本地缓存本政策所述的订阅信息。当前源代码审计未发现第三方广告 SDK 或第三方分析 SDK；发布前仍应再次检查依赖和 App Store 隐私表单。

保存与删除

本地信息通常会保留至你在 App 内删除单个数据，或移除 App。CloudKit 信息遵循单项删除/同步行为及 Apple 的 iCloud 管理和账户控制；当前 App 没有提供全面清除 CloudKit 数据的控制项。当前 AI 服务端代码不持久化 App 数据，并请求服务提供方不存储，但基础设施和服务提供方日志的保存情况尚未确认。如需了解可用选项，请联系我们。

儿童、更新与联系

拳伴并非面向会被我们明知收集个人信息的儿童。如果你认为儿童曾通过 App 提供信息，请联系我们。我们可能更新本政策，并在此页更新日期。
